Introduction

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Electronics-ICT (Internet of Things), which I completed with distinction in 2025 at AP Hogeschool in Antwerp.

While my education has a strong hardware foundation, I discovered that software development—and particularly coding—is where my true passion lies. I am versatile, eager to tackle diverse challenges, and always prepared to learn new skills.

My experience spans a wide range: from full-stack development using tools like Angular, Java and Dot Net, to embedded systems development where I’ve designed PCBs and developed firmware for various microcontrollers. In addition, I have a solid understanding of how pointers and references work in C++, as well as experience with the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture. I’m also familiar with building backend APIs that follow a strict layered structure combined with a ports-and-adapters (hexagonal) architecture, ensuring a clean separation between business logic and external interfaces.
